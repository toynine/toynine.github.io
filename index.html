What it does

Most sleep apps stop playing in the middle of the night. Nooma was built to fix exactly that: your sounds keep going in the background, with the screen off, all night long.

Features

  • Uninterrupted playback — screen off, all night, no sudden stops
  • 44 sounds — white, pink and brown noise, rain, waves, womb sounds, fireplace, lo-fi and more
  • Mixer — layer sounds and set the volume of each one
  • Sleep timer — set any duration, with a gentle fade-out
  • Favorites — save your perfect mix and open it with one tap
  • Free and offline — no paywall, no sign-in, all sounds bundled
  • 9 languages

Who it's for

Parents settling an infant at night, adults who sleep better with steady background sound, and anyone who needs a constant ambience to focus, study or relax.
